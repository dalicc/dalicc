## What a commercial licence typically covers

* use of the software without the obligations of the AGPL;
* the right to modify it and to keep those modifications private;
* deployment as a network service inside your organisation or for your customers,
  without the source offer of AGPL section 13;
* optionally, a support and maintenance arrangement.

  receive a licence, not ownership.

  components DALICC builds on, which keep their own licences in every case.
